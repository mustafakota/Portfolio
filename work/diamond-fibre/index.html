01. Brand Background & Manufacturing Heritage

Diamond Fibre is an established family-owned bedding manufacturer operating out of Ratlam, Madhya Pradesh. For decades, the business built a reputable regional trade manufacturing four core categories: pure cotton gaddas (traditional Indian mattresses), boxed-edge orthopedic mattresses, virgin microfiber pillows, and heavy winter razais (quilts) and mink blankets.

While the factory had strong production capacity and longstanding regional wholesale trust, their digital presence was nonexistent. Commercial resellers, boutique hotels, and retail shoppers in nearby districts had no central place to view product specifications, inspect mattress layer constructions, or initiate direct bulk inquiries.

02. The Design & Business Objectives

The primary challenge was designing a digital experience that solved two distinct visitor intents without confusing either:

AUDIENCE 01

Retail Bedding Shoppers

Homeowners seeking authentic cotton mattresses, customized bed sizes, and transparent material quality without middleman markups.

AUDIENCE 02

B2B Wholesale & Hospitality

Hotels, regional furniture showrooms, and institutional buyers requiring bulk volume pricing, custom branding, and reliable production timelines.

03. Information Architecture & Product Categorization

Rather than building a complex, high-friction e-commerce cart that would detach users from personal customer consultation, I structured the site around a rich, interactive catalog:

  • Pure Cotton Gaddas: Highlighting virgin cotton batting, traditional tufting techniques, and customizable dimensions for standard bed sizes.
  • Boxed-Edge Mattresses: Visualizing layered multi-density foam and pocket-spring cores tailored for orthopedic spinal alignment.
  • Fiber Pillows: Showcasing hypoallergenic microfiber fillings, breathable cotton casings, and pack-size options.
  • Winter Razais & Blankets: Thermal quilt warmth ratings, hand-stitched borders, and luxury mink blankets for seasonal hospitality demands.

04. Frontend Development & Mobile-First Execution

A large percentage of prospective wholesale buyers and retail customers access the web via mobile devices in regional network conditions. I built the site using lightweight semantic HTML, responsive CSS, and performant asset loading:

  • Zero Stock Photo Artifice: Used authentic photographs captured at the Ratlam production facility, reinforcing local manufacturer authenticity.
  • Direct WhatsApp & Call Inquiries: Sticky, non-intrusive quick-action buttons allowing immediate consultation with the factory sales desk.
  • High Performance: Optimized image payloads ensuring sub-second visual rendering on 4G networks across Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities.

The production website is accessible live at diamondfibre.shop.
